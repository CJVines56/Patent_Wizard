## Abstract

A combined cycle power generation facility of an embodiment includes a first heat recovery steam generator including a high-pressure steam generation part using an exhaust gas of a gas turbine and a reheat part; a high-pressure turbine to which steam is introduced from the high-pressure steam generation part; a combustor which combusts hydrogen and oxygen; a reheat steam pipe connecting the high-pressure turbine and the combustor with the reheat part interposed therebetween; an oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine to which steam is introduced from the combustor; a second heat recovery steam generator including a steam generation part using an exhaust gas of the oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine; a low-pressure turbine to which steam is introduced from the steam generation part; and a condenser which steam is introduced from the low-pressure turbine.

## Description

This application is based upon and claims the benefit of priority from Japanese Patent Application No. 2022-130462, filed on Aug. 18, 2022; the entire contents of which are incorporated herein by reference.

Embodiments of the present invention relate to a combined cycle power generation facility.

In a thermal power plant in recent years, a demand for a combined cycle power generation facility to yield high thermal efficiency is increasing. A conventional combined cycle power generation facility includes a gas turbine, a heat recovery steam generator, a steam turbine, and a generator. In this combined cycle power generation facility, an exhaust gas at high temperatures from the gas turbine is introduced to the heat recovery steam generator. Steam generated in the heat recovery steam generator is introduced to the steam turbine.

In the heat recovery steam generator of the conventional combined cycle power generation facility, a temperature of the generated steam is lower than a temperature of the exhaust gas of the gas turbine. Further, there is a limit to further increasing a temperature of the steam to be introduced to the steam turbine under a condition where the temperature of the exhaust gas of the conventional gas turbine is maintained. Therefore, the thermal efficiency of the cycle is difficult to further improve under the condition where the temperature of the exhaust gas of the conventional gas turbine is maintained.

In one embodiment, a combined cycle power generation facility of an embodiment includes: a gas turbine; a first heat recovery steam generator including a first steam generation part which uses a heat quantity of an exhaust gas from the gas turbine to generate steam, and a reheat part which reheats steam; and a first steam turbine to which the steam generated by the first steam generation part is introduced.

Further, the combined cycle power generation facility includes: a combustor which combusts hydrogen and oxygen; a reheat steam pipe connecting a steam outlet of the first steam turbine and the combustor with the reheat part interposed therebetween; a second steam turbine which is coupled to the combustor and to which steam discharged from the combustor is introduced; a second heat recovery steam generator including a second steam generation part which uses a heat quantity of steam discharged from the second steam turbine to generate steam; a third steam turbine to which the steam generated by the second steam generation part is introduced; and a condenser which changes steam discharged from the third steam turbine into condensed water.

FIG. <b>1</b> is a system diagram schematically illustrating a configuration of a combined cycle power generation facility <b>1</b> of the embodiment. As illustrated in FIG. <b>1</b>, the combined cycle power generation facility <b>1</b> includes a gas turbine <b>10</b>, a steam turbine system <b>70</b>, and a generator <b>20</b> as main constituent apparatuses.

The gas turbine <b>10</b> includes a compressor <b>11</b>, a combustor <b>12</b>, and a turbine <b>13</b>. The generator <b>20</b> is disposed on the same axis as the compressor <b>11</b> and the turbine <b>13</b>. The respective rotors of the compressor <b>11</b>, the turbine <b>13</b>, and the generator <b>20</b> are configured to integrally rotate.

The compressor <b>11</b> sucks and compresses air to supply it to the combustor <b>12</b>, for example. A fuel supply part <b>14</b> supplies fuel to the combustor <b>12</b>. In the combustor <b>12</b>, the fuel and the compressed air are combusted, and a combustion gas is produced.

Note that an example of using the compressed air as an oxidant is indicated here, but the oxidant is not limited to air. As the oxidant, for example, oxygen may be used. The fuel is not particularly limited. As the fuel, for example, hydrocarbon such as methane or natural gas, hydrogen, ammonia, a composite fuel of hydrocarbon and hydrogen, a composite fuel of hydrocarbon and ammonia, or the like may be used. Further, as the fuel, for example, a coal gasification gas fuel containing carbon monoxide, hydrogen, and the like, or a coal gasification gas fuel containing ammonia may be used.

The combustion gas produced by the combustor <b>12</b> is supplied to the turbine <b>13</b> to rotationally move the turbine <b>13</b>. The generator <b>20</b> and the compressor <b>11</b> are driven by rotationally moving the turbine <b>13</b>. The combustion gas discharged from the turbine <b>13</b> is guided to a first heat recovery steam generator <b>30</b> as an exhaust gas.

The steam turbine system <b>70</b> includes the first heat recovery steam generator (HRSG) <b>30</b>, a plurality of steam turbines, a combustor <b>75</b>, a second heat recovery steam generator (HRSG) <b>80</b>, and a condenser <b>74</b>. Here, an action in the steam turbine system <b>70</b> will be explained.

The steam introduced to the high-pressure turbine <b>71</b> via the main steam pipe <b>60</b> rotationally moves the high-pressure turbine <b>71</b> to be thereafter discharged to the low-temperature reheat steam pipe <b>64</b>. The steam discharged to the low-temperature reheat steam pipe <b>64</b> is guided to the reheat part <b>50</b>. At this time, for example, the steam is introduced from the intermediate-pressure superheater <b>42</b><i>a </i>via the steam pipe <b>62</b> to the low-temperature reheat steam pipe <b>64</b>.

The steam guided to the reheat part <b>50</b> via the low-temperature reheat steam pipe <b>64</b> is reheated, and guided to the high-temperature reheat steam pipe <b>65</b>. Then, the steam is introduced to the combustor <b>75</b> via the high-temperature reheat steam pipe <b>65</b>.

In the combustor <b>75</b>, water vapor at high temperatures is produced by the combustion of hydrogen and oxygen. The water vapor produced by the combustor <b>75</b> is mixed with the steam introduced to the combustor <b>75</b> via the high-temperature reheat steam pipe <b>65</b> to become the steam to be introduced to the oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine <b>72</b>. A temperature of the steam introduced to the combustor <b>75</b> rises by being mixed with the water vapor produced by the combustor <b>75</b>. That is, steam with a temperature exceeding a temperature of the steam reheated in the reheat part <b>50</b> is introduced to the oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine <b>72</b> by including the combustor <b>75</b>.

The steam introduced to the oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine <b>72</b> rotationally moves the oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine <b>72</b> to be thereafter introduced to the second heat recovery steam generator <b>80</b> via the steam pipe <b>102</b>. The steam generated by the steam generation part <b>90</b> of the second heat recovery steam generator <b>80</b> is introduced to the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> via the steam pipe <b>100</b>. The steam introduced to the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> rotationally moves the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> to be thereafter discharged to the exhaust pipe <b>66</b>.

Note that a state of the steam to be introduced to the oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine <b>72</b> is regulated by setting of a supply heat quantity in the combustor <b>75</b>, and the like. A state of the steam discharged from the oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine <b>72</b> is regulated by setting of a configuration of the oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine <b>72</b>, the supply heat quantity in the combustor <b>75</b>, and the like. A pressure and a temperature of the steam to be introduced to the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> are regulated by a state of the steam to be introduced to the second heat recovery steam generator <b>80</b>, a configuration of the steam generation part <b>90</b>, and the like.

Here, the pressure of the steam to be introduced to the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> via the steam pipe <b>100</b> is set to be higher than a pressure of the steam discharged from the oxygen-hydrogen combustion turbine <b>72</b>. Note that states of steam from a steam inlet of the combustor <b>75</b> to the steam outlet of the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> will be explained in detail later.

The steam discharged from the low-pressure superheater <b>43</b><i>a </i>is guided to a predetermined turbine stage of the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> via the steam supply pipe <b>61</b>. Moreover, the steam discharged from the second heat recovery steam generator <b>80</b> is guided to a predetermined turbine stage of the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> via the steam pipe <b>101</b> and the steam supply pipe <b>61</b>. A turbine output is increased by introducing the steam discharged from the low-pressure superheater <b>43</b><i>a </i>and the steam discharged from the second heat recovery steam generator <b>80</b> to the predetermined turbine stage.

The steam introduced to the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> rotationally moves the low-pressure turbine <b>73</b> to be thereafter discharged to the discharge pipe <b>66</b>. The steam discharged to the exhaust pipe <b>66</b> is introduced to the condenser <b>74</b>, and changed into the condensed water.

The condensed water in the condenser <b>74</b> is pumped by the low-pressure feed pump <b>76</b> and the high-pressure feed pumps <b>77</b><i>a</i>, <b>77</b><i>b</i>, and guided to the high-pressure economizer <b>41</b><i>e</i>, the low-pressure economizer <b>43</b><i>d</i>, and the economizer <b>90</b><i>d </i>via the feed pipes <b>67</b>, <b>67</b><i>a</i>, <b>67</b><i>b</i>, as previously described.
