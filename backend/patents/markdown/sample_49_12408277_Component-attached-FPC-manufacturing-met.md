## Abstract

A component-attached FPC manufacturing method for manufacturing a component-attached FPC in which a circuit component is solder-bonded to an FPC, includes an FPC forming step of forming an FPC that integrally includes an FPC body, an FPC arm portion branching off from the FPC body, a component mounting portion provided on an end portion of the FPC arm portion and on which the circuit component is mounted, and a bridge connecting the component mounting portion and the FPC body, a component mounting step of mounting the circuit component on the component mounting portion via a solder, and an implementing step of heating the FPC, melting the solder, and implementing the circuit component on the component mounting portion.

## Description

The present disclosure relates to a component-attached FPC manufacturing method.

For example, Patent Document 1 discloses a technique to mount chip components on a flexible substrate by placing the flexible substrate on a tray, placing the chip components on the flexible substrate, and mounting the chip components on the flexible substrate by reflow deposition.

One example of a technique for mounting chip components on a flexible substrate by reflow deposition is a technique of mounting chip components on the flexible substrate by reflow deposition upon placing a plate having an opening on the flexible substrate to expose the chip component through the opening and pressing the flexible substrate with the plate in order to prevent the flexible substrate from warping.

[Patent Document 1] JPH06120653 A

However, in a conventional method of mounting chip components on a flexible substrate, as illustrated in FIGS. <b>11</b>, <b>12</b>, when chip components <b>102</b> are mounted on a portion exposed from an opening <b>152</b><i>a </i>of a plate <b>152</b>, i.e., a portion <b>103</b>A branching off from a flexible substrate (FPC) <b>103</b>, during reflow deposition, there was a concern that the portion <b>103</b>A on which the chip component <b>102</b> is mounted may warp, causing the chip component (circuit component) <b>102</b> to be mounted in an out-of-position state, i.e., resulting in a mounting failure.

It is an object of the present disclosure to provide a component-attached FPC manufacturing method that allows a circuit component to be mounted at predetermined location branched off from an FPC body.

According to an aspect of the present disclosure, a component-attached FPC manufacturing method for manufacturing a component-attached FPC in which a circuit component is solder-bonded to an FPC, includes an FPC forming step of forming an FPC that integrally includes an FPC body, an FPC arm portion branching off from the FPC body, a component mounting portion provided on an end portion of the FPC arm portion and on which the circuit component is mounted, and a bridge connecting the component mounting portion and the FPC body, a component mounting step of mounting the circuit component on the component mounting portion via a solder, and an implementing step of heating the FPC, melting the solder, and implementing the circuit component on the component mounting portion.

According to another aspect of the present disclosure, a component-attached FPC manufacturing method for manufacturing a component-attached FPC in which a circuit component is solder-bonded to an FPC, includes an FPC forming step of forming an FPC that integrally includes an FPC body, an FPC arm portion branching off from the FPC body, a component mounting portion provided on an end portion of the FPC arm portion and on which the circuit component is mounted, and an extension portion extending from a peripheral edge of the component mounting portion, a component mounting step of mounting the circuit component on the component mounting portion via a solder, a plate mounting step of mounting a plate such that at least a portion of the extension portion is covered and the component mounting portion, and an implementing step of heating the FPC, melting the solder, and implementing the circuit component on the component mounting portion.

According to the present disclosure, a circuit component can be mounted at predetermined location branched off from an FPC body.

Hereinafter, a component-attached FPC manufacturing method <b>1</b> according to the first embodiment of the present disclosure is explained with reference to FIGS. <b>1</b> to <b>6</b>. As illustrated in FIG. <b>1</b>, the component-attached FPC <b>10</b> manufactured by the component-attached FPC manufacturing method <b>1</b> includes a circuit component <b>2</b> and an FPC <b>3</b> (Flexible Printed Circuits) to which the circuit component <b>2</b> is solder-bonded. In the present embodiment, the arrows X, Y, and Z are directions perpendicular to each other. The arrows X and Y indicate the planar direction in which the FPC <b>3</b> extends, and the arrow Z indicates the direction in which the circuit component <b>2</b> is mounted relative to the FPC <b>3</b>.

The circuit component <b>2</b> is solder-bonded to a component mounting portion <b>33</b> of the FPC <b>3</b> (described later) and is electrically connected to a circuit formed in the FPC <b>3</b>. In this case, the circuit component <b>2</b> is an element that performs various functions. Examples of the circuit component <b>2</b> include a connector, a capacitor, a relay, a resistor, a transistor, an IPS (Intelligent Power Switch), and an electronic control unit including a microcomputer. As illustrated in FIG. <b>1</b>, the circuit component <b>2</b> includes a circuit component body (not illustrated) and multiple connection portions <b>22</b> extending from both ends of the circuit component body (in the figure, only one of the multiple connection portions <b>22</b> is illustrated, and the others are omitted). In the following, one of the multiple connection portions <b>22</b> may be referred to as a “first connection portion” and the others may be referred to as “second connection portions”.

In the present embodiment, the first connection portion <b>22</b> is constituted by a conductive metal tab in a rectangular plate shape. The first connection portion <b>22</b> is disposed so that a portion of the first connection portion <b>22</b> extends from the component mounting portion <b>33</b> provided on the FPC <b>3</b>, which will be described later.

As illustrated in FIGS. <b>1</b>, <b>3</b>, the FPC <b>3</b> integrally includes an FPC body <b>31</b> formed in a rectangular shape, an FPC arm portion <b>32</b> branching off from the FPC body <b>31</b>, a component mounting portion <b>33</b> which is provided on the end portion of the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> and on which a first connection portion <b>22</b> of a circuit component <b>2</b> is mounted, and a bridge <b>34</b> connecting the component mounting portion <b>33</b> and the FPC body <b>31</b>.

As illustrated in FIG. <b>3</b>, the FPC body <b>31</b> is formed in a rectangular shape. The FPC body <b>31</b> includes a pair of first edges <b>31</b><i>a</i>, <b>31</b><i>a </i>that form both ends of the FPC body <b>31</b> in the left-right direction X and extend in the front-rear direction Y, and a second edge <b>31</b><i>b </i>that is continuous with the pair of first edges <b>31</b><i>a</i>, <b>31</b><i>a </i>and extends in the left-right direction X.

As illustrated in FIG. <b>3</b>, the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> includes an FPC arm portion body <b>321</b> in a strip shape extending to the front side Y<b>1</b> from the second edge <b>31</b><i>b </i>of the FPC body <b>31</b>, and an FPC extension portion <b>322</b> in a strip shape bent at a right angle at an end of the FPC arm portion body <b>321</b> away from the FPC body <b>31</b> and extending in the left-and-right direction X. One end <b>32</b>L of the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> is continuous with the second edge <b>31</b><i>b </i>of the FPC body <b>31</b>, and the other end <b>32</b>R is continuous with a first peripheral edge <b>33</b><i>a </i>of a component mounting portion <b>33</b> described below.

As illustrated in FIG. <b>3</b>, the component mounting portion <b>33</b> is formed in a rectangular shape. The component mounting portion <b>33</b> includes a first peripheral edge <b>33</b><i>a </i>and a second peripheral edge <b>33</b><i>b </i>extending in the front-and-rear direction Y, and a third peripheral edge <b>33</b><i>c </i>and a fourth peripheral edge <b>33</b><i>d </i>extending in the left-and-right direction X. Furthermore, the component mounting portion <b>33</b> is provided on the opposite side of the FPC extension portion <b>322</b> from the FPC arm portion body <b>321</b> in left-and-right direction X. Solder printing is applied to the upper surface of the component mounting portion <b>33</b>. In the following, solder is given a reference numeral <b>4</b>. Further, the component mounting portion <b>33</b> is configured so that the first connection portion <b>22</b> of the circuit component <b>2</b> is electrically connected to the component mounting portion <b>33</b> via the solder <b>4</b>. That is, the circuit component <b>2</b> is mounted on the component mounting portion <b>33</b>.

The bridge <b>34</b> is formed in a strip shape as illustrated in FIG. <b>3</b>. The bridge <b>34</b> is situated between the FPC body <b>31</b> and the component mounting portion <b>33</b> and is provided continuously with both of them. The bridge <b>34</b> extends in the front-and-rear direction Y, with one end continuing to the second edge <b>31</b><i>b </i>of the FPC body <b>31</b> and the other end continuing to the third peripheral edge <b>33</b><i>c </i>of the component mounting portion <b>33</b>.

Next, a manufacturing apparatus <b>5</b> used in the manufacturing the component-attached FPC <b>10</b> is explained with reference to FIG. <b>4</b>.

As illustrated in FIGS. <b>3</b> and <b>4</b>, the manufacturing apparatus <b>5</b> includes a tray <b>51</b> on which the FPC <b>3</b> is placed, a reflow furnace (not illustrated) that melts the solder <b>4</b> by heating, and a plate <b>52</b> (illustrated in FIG. <b>4</b>) that covers and presses the FPC <b>3</b>.

As illustrated in FIG. <b>4</b>, the tray <b>51</b> is configured so that the FPC <b>3</b> and the circuit component <b>2</b> can be placed thereon. The tray <b>51</b> is formed in a flat plate shape and is configured to have an area larger than that of the component-attached FPC <b>10</b>.

As illustrated in FIG. <b>4</b>, the plate <b>52</b> includes a plate body <b>521</b> that is of such a size as to cover the FPC body <b>31</b> and a portion of the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> of the FPC <b>3</b>, and an opening <b>522</b> in a rectangular shape provided in the plate body <b>521</b>. The opening <b>522</b> is of such a size as to expose the component mounting portion <b>33</b>, a portion of the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> including the other end <b>32</b>R, and the first connection portion <b>22</b> of the circuit component <b>2</b>.

Next, a method for manufacturing the component-attached FPC <b>10</b> using the manufacturing apparatus <b>5</b> (the component-attached FPC manufacturing method <b>1</b>) is explained with reference to FIGS. <b>2</b> to <b>6</b>.

First, as illustrated in FIGS. <b>2</b>, <b>3</b>, the FPC <b>3</b> is formed that integrally includes the FPC body <b>31</b>, the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> branching off from the FPC body <b>31</b>, the component mounting portion <b>33</b> provided on the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> and on which the circuit component <b>2</b> is mounted, and the bridge <b>34</b> connecting the component mounting portion <b>33</b> and the FPC body <b>31</b> (FPC forming step S<b>1</b>). Then, solder is printed on the upper surface of the component mounting portion <b>33</b>. Then, the FPC <b>3</b> is placed in the tray <b>51</b>.

Next, as illustrated in FIG. <b>4</b>, the first connection portion <b>22</b> of the circuit component <b>2</b> are mounted on the component mounting portion <b>33</b> (component mounting step S<b>2</b>). The first connection portion <b>22</b> is placed such that a portion on the rear side Y<b>2</b> is on the component mounting portion <b>33</b>, and that a portion on the front side Y<b>1</b> crosses the fourth peripheral edge <b>33</b><i>d </i>of the component mounting portion <b>33</b>. In this manner, the circuit component <b>2</b> is mounted on the component mounting portion <b>33</b> via the solder <b>4</b>.

Next, as illustrated in FIG. <b>4</b>, the plate <b>52</b> is placed on the FPC <b>3</b> (plate mounting step S<b>3</b>). The plate <b>52</b> is placed such that the plate body <b>521</b> covers the FPC body <b>31</b> and a portion of the FPC arm portion <b>32</b>, and that a portion of the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> including the other end <b>32</b>R, the component mounting portion <b>33</b>, the first connection portion <b>22</b> of the circuit component <b>2</b>, and the bridge <b>34</b> are exposed from the opening <b>522</b>. In the following, as illustrated in FIG. <b>4</b>, a state in which the FPC <b>3</b> and the circuit component <b>2</b> are placed on the tray <b>51</b> and the plate <b>52</b> is placed at a predetermined position on the FPC <b>3</b> may be referred to as a “component set <b>6</b>”. If there is no risk of warping of the FPC body <b>31</b>, the plate <b>52</b> may be omitted.

Next, the component set <b>6</b> is placed in the reflow furnace. Accordingly, the FPC <b>3</b> is heated, and the solder <b>4</b> is melted. In this case, the component mounting portion <b>33</b> is supported on the FPC body <b>31</b> by the bridge <b>34</b>, and warping of the component mounting portion <b>33</b> is alleviated.

Thereafter, the first connection portion <b>22</b> of the circuit component <b>2</b> is electrically connected to the circuit formed in component mounting portion <b>33</b> of the FPC <b>3</b> via the solder <b>4</b>. Also, second connection portion of circuit component <b>2</b> is electrically connected to the circuit at a predetermined position. Accordingly, the circuit component <b>2</b> is mounted on the component mounting portion <b>33</b> (implementing step S<b>4</b>). In this case, warping of the component mounting portion <b>33</b> is alleviated, so that the circuit component <b>2</b> is positioned at a predetermined position on the component mounting portion <b>33</b>.

Thereafter, the component set <b>6</b> is removed from the reflow furnace, and the plate <b>52</b> is removed. Then, as illustrated in FIG. <b>5</b>, the bridge <b>34</b> is cut at a boundary position L<b>1</b> between the bridge <b>34</b> and the FPC body <b>31</b> and at a boundary position L<b>2</b> between the bridge <b>34</b> and the component mounting portion <b>33</b> (bridge cutting step S<b>5</b>). In this manner, the component-attached FPC <b>10</b> is completed as illustrated in FIG. <b>6</b>.

According to the embodiment explained above, the component-attached FPC manufacturing method <b>1</b> for manufacturing the component-attached FPC <b>10</b> in which the circuit component <b>2</b> is solder-bonded to the FPC <b>3</b> includes an FPC forming step S<b>1</b> of forming the FPC <b>3</b> that integrally includes the FPC body <b>31</b>, the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> branching off from the FPC body <b>31</b>, the component mounting portion <b>33</b> provided on the end of the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> and on which the circuit component <b>2</b> is mounted, and the bridge <b>34</b> connecting the component mounting portion <b>33</b> and the FPC body <b>31</b>. Accordingly, when the FPC <b>3</b> is heated, the FPC <b>3</b> is heated in the reflow furnace while the component mounting portion <b>33</b> is supported on the FPC body <b>31</b> by the bridge <b>34</b>, so that warping of the component mounting portion <b>33</b> is alleviated. As a result, the circuit component <b>2</b> can be mounted in a predetermined orientation at the predetermined position (the component mounting portion <b>33</b>) branched off from the FPC body <b>31</b>.

The plate mounting step S<b>3</b> is provided to place the plate <b>52</b> such as to cover a portion of the FPC body <b>31</b> and a portion of the FPC arm portion <b>32</b> while exposing the component mounting portion <b>33</b>. As a result, warping of the FPC body <b>31</b> is alleviated.

The bridge cutting step S<b>5</b> of cutting the bridge <b>34</b> is provided, and the bridge cutting step S<b>5</b> is performed after the implementing step S<b>4</b>. Accordingly, the unnecessary portion can be cut off to produce the component-attached FPC <b>10</b> that is light in weight.
