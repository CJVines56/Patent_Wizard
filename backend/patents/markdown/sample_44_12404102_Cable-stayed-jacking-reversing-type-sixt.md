## Abstract

A cable-stayed jacking reversing type sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle comprises a supporting frame, a transverse movement drive mechanism, a longitudinal movement drive mechanism and a cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism, wherein the transverse movement drive mechanism includes transverse driving wheels and first/second/third transverse driven wheels; the longitudinal movement drive mechanism includes first/second longitudinal driving wheels and first/second longitudinal driven wheels; and, the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism includes jacking transverse plates and cable-stayed jacking blocks, in which a ball bearing sleeve is movably arranged in a jacking shaft hole, a cable-stayed jacking shaft is arranged between the front and rear sides of the ball bearing sleeve, and the cable-stayed jacking blocks are connected with each other through ball screws. The shuttle is a sixteen-wheel two-way moving vehicle which is compact, cost-effective and space saving, and which also greatly improves the carrying capacity and obstacle crossing ability.

## Description

The present disclosure relates to the technical field of shuttles, and in particular, to a cable-stayed jacking reversing type sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle.

With a rapid development of warehousing and logistics industry, a three-dimensional warehouse space with higher a utilization rate is required. For the three-dimensional warehouse, it is hoped that more shelves should be arranged in the horizontal direction and more shelf compartments should be arranged in the height direction, so as to minimize the space occupied by incoming and unloading passages.

The current three-dimensional warehouse structure prefers to use the type of one incoming and unloading lane and multiple racking lanes vertically connected to it. Thus, it should be needed that a shuttle must be able to move in two mutually perpendicular directions, longitudinally and transversely. In the prior art, the three-dimensional warehouse usually uses a sub-mother vehicle system, that is, a main vehicle carries a sub-vehicle and moves in the incoming and unloading lane, and then the sub-vehicle drives out in a direction perpendicular to the direction of the main vehicle. The sub-vehicle enters in racking lanes, and then goes back along the original line to the main vehicle, and finally is taken away by the main vehicle.

The existing shuttle in the type of the sub-mother vehicle system mostly uses a two-way eight-wheel structure, which has the disadvantages of low carrying capacity, weak obstacle crossing capacity and high failure rate, thus it can not meet the increasingly busy warehousing and logistics needs. Moreover, it generally uses a cam jacking structure, which has the defects of easy wear and tear, uneven force on the follower and low transmission efficiency. In addition, the existing shuttle of the sub-mother vehicle system not only occupies a considerable space, reducing the number of shelves and compartments and the space utilization of the three-dimensional warehouse, but also increases the cost of manufacturing, procurement and maintenance.

Embodiments of the present disclosure provide a cable-stayed jacking reversing type sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle.

Detailed technical solutions are as follows:

The embodiments of the present disclosure provide a cable-stayed jacking reversing type sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle, comprising a support frame, a transverse movement drive mechanism, a longitudinal movement drive mechanism and a cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism, in which the mechanisms are all mounted on the support frame, wherein:

    
    
        the transverse movement drive mechanism includes transverse driving wheels, first transverse driven wheels, second transverse driven wheels and third transverse driven wheels, in which each set of the wheels is symmetrically arranged on the front and rear sides of the support frame; and, the two transverse driving wheels are respectively arranged on both ends of a transverse transmission shaft, the two third transverse driven wheels are respectively arranged on both ends of a transverse connecting shaft, and the transverse transmission shaft is drivingly connected to a transverse motor through a first transverse transmission belt;
        the longitudinal movement drive mechanism includes first longitudinal driving wheels, second longitudinal driving wheels, first longitudinal driven wheels and second longitudinal driven wheels, in which each set of the wheels is symmetrically arranged on the left and right sides of the support frame; and, the first longitudinal driving wheels and the second longitudinal driving wheels on both sides are connected to a longitudinal transmission shaft through first longitudinal transmission belts on the corresponding side, and the longitudinal transmission shaft is drivingly connected to a longitudinal motor through a second longitudinal transmission belt; and,
        the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism includes jacking transverse plates symmetrically arranged on the front and rear sides of the support frame, and cable-stayed jacking blocks respectively arranged at the bottoms of both ends of each jacking transverse plate; each end of the jacking transverse plate is provided with a first reversing assembling groove and a second reversing assembling groove, to assemble the transverse transmission shaft and the transverse connecting shaft; each cable-stayed jacking block is provided with a jacking shaft hole penetrating left and right and a jacking inclined hole arranging obliquely and penetrating the front and rear; a ball bearing sleeve is movably arranged in the jacking shaft hole, a cable-stayed jacking shaft is arranged between the front and rear sides of the ball bearing sleeve, and the cable-stayed jacking shaft is movably embedded in the jacking inclined hole; and, a ball screw is movably penetrated in each ball bearing sleeve, the cable-stayed jacking blocks at both ends are connected with each other through the ball screws, and the ball screws are drivingly connected to the transverse motor through a chain.

In some embodiments, the front and rear side walls of the support frame are respectively provided with four square mounting holes, and the left and right side walls of the support frame are respectively provided with four circular mounting holes; wherein:

    
    
        the transverse driving wheels are connected with the transverse transmission shaft which passes through the second reversing assembling groove to assemble into the corresponding square assembling holes; the axles of the first transverse driven wheels and the second transverse driven wheels respectively pass through the corresponding square mounting holes to connect with the corresponding jacking transverse plate; and, the third transverse driven wheels are connected with the transverse connecting shaft which passes through the first reversing assembling groove to assemble into the corresponding square assembling holes; and,
        the axles of the first longitudinal driving wheels and the second longitudinal driving wheels, which respectively pass through the corresponding circular mounting holes, are connected to the bottom of the supporting frame through their corresponding first bearing seats; and the first longitudinal driven wheels and the second longitudinal driven wheels are respectively assembled into the corresponding circular assembling holes.

In some embodiments, the transverse driving wheel and the first transverse driven wheel on the same side are connected with each other through a second transverse transmission belt; and/or, the transverse driving wheel and the third transverse driven wheel on the same side are connected with each other through the second transverse transmission belt.

In some embodiments, the cable-stayed jacking reversing type sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle further comprises a tension adjusting mechanism positioned outside the first longitudinal transmission belt, wherein:

    
    
        the tension adjusting mechanism includes a first tensioning pulley and a second tensioning pulley which are both attached to the outside of the first longitudinal transmission belt, the first tensioning pulley is fixedly arranged on the inner side wall of the support frame through a first fixing plate, and the second tensioning pulley is movably arranged on the inner side wall of the support frame through an adjustment plate.

In some embodiments, the adjusting plate is provided with an adjusting square hole in horizontal, and the adjusting plate is movably fixed to the inner side wall of the supporting frame by bolts passing through the adjusting square hole; and, one end of the adjusting plate is adjustably connected to a second fixing plate fixed on the inner side wall of the support frame by adjustable nuts and bolts.

In some embodiments, the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism further comprises:

    
    
        second bearing seats, each arranged on the support frame with a bearing arranged at its top to support the corresponding ball screw; and,
        jacking nuts, each fixedly sleeved on the corresponding ball bearing sleeve with its front and rear side walls respectively welded and connected to the cable-stayed jacking shaft.

In some embodiments, the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism further comprises:

    
    
        limiting sliding grooves, each vertically arranged on the front and rear side walls of the cable-stayed jacking block; and,
        limiting guide posts, each vertically arranged on the support frame and slidably connected with the corresponding limiting sliding grooves.

In some embodiments, each jacking transverse plate is slidably connected to the corresponding jacking guide posts through guide sliding grooves opened at both ends of the jacking transverse plate, and the jacking guide posts are located at the four corners of the support frame.

In some embodiments, the inclination angle of the jacking inclined hole is 5-85°, and the vertical height of the jacking inclined hole is equal to the vertical height of the jacking shaft hole.

In some embodiments, the transverse motor is respectively drivingly connected to the first transverse transmission belt and the chain through a cross reducer.

The present disclosure achieves the following beneficial effects:

    
    
        (1) In the present disclosure, the use of eight transverse wheels, eight longitudinal wheels and a reinforced support frame can improve the loading capacity; and the installation position of each wheel is optimized so as to achieve a better obstacle crossing ability;
        (2) In order to match up with sixteen wheels, the wheel transmission mechanism is optimized; and, by moving the installation position of the longitudinal transmission shaft upwards and increasing the diameter of the longitudinal wheels, the shuttle can have a better loading capacity;
        (3) The tension adjusting mechanism is used to adjust the tension of the first longitudinal transmission belt, while ensuring the stability and continuity of the power transmission of the first longitudinal driving wheels and the second longitudinal driving wheels;
        (4) In the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism, the cable-stayed jacking blocks contact to the rolling line of the cable-stayed jacking shaft, with high transmission efficiency and no change in the pressure angle; and, the jacking inclined holes on both sides of the jacking block are symmetrically arranged with uniform force. The afore design is novel with compact structure, stable operation, small size and high loading capacity, thereby reducing the maintenance and maintenance times of the mechanical mechanism so as to have high work efficiency and long service life;
        (5) The transmission structure with the fitted ball screws and ball bearing sleeves makes the transmission efficiency reach 50%-70%, and the ball bearing sleeves and the jacking shaft are directly connected by jacking nuts, which reduces the loss of kinetic energy;
        (6) The sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle has a compact structure, which not only reduces costs and saves space, but also reduces the overall weight and volume of the vehicle and improves operating capacity and cargo storage efficiency.

Referring to FIG. <b>1</b>, an embodiment of the present disclosure provides a cable-stayed jacking reversing type sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle, comprising a support frame <b>100</b>, a transverse movement drive mechanism <b>200</b>, a longitudinal movement drive mechanism <b>300</b>, a cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b>, a battery pack <b>500</b> and a controller <b>600</b>, in which the mechanisms, the battery pack and the controller are all mounted on the support frame <b>100</b>.

In one example, the supporting frame <b>100</b> is an integral frame formed by the connecting between a bending sheet metal and local reinforcing ribs, which greatly improves the carrying capacity of the vehicle. The battery pack <b>500</b> is used as a power source of the vehicle, and the battery pack <b>500</b> can use lithium-ion rechargeable batteries, storage batteries or any other energy storage batteries to supply power for each function unit of the vehicle. The controller <b>600</b> is a key core component of the whole vehicle, which controls the movement of the vehicle. The controller <b>600</b> is electrically connected to the battery pack <b>500</b>, a transverse motor <b>208</b> of the transverse movement drive mechanism <b>200</b>, a longitudinal motor <b>307</b> of the longitudinal movement mechanism <b>300</b> and a jacking motor <b>408</b> of the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b>. The flexibly longitudinal or transverse movement of the vehicle is controlled by the controller <b>600</b>, and the jacking and reversing of the vehicle is achieved by the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b>.

In some preferred embodiments, referring to FIG. <b>2</b> and FIG. <b>4</b>, the transverse movement drive mechanism <b>200</b> acts as an execution unit for a transverse movement of the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle. The transverse movement drive mechanism <b>200</b> includes transverse driving wheels <b>201</b>, first transverse driven wheels <b>202</b>, second transverse driven wheels <b>203</b> and third transverse driven wheels <b>204</b>, in which each set of the wheels is symmetrically arranged on the front and rear sides of the support frame <b>100</b>. Each set of the wheels has two wheels symmetrically arranged on the front and rear sides of the support frame <b>100</b> to form a structure of a one-way eight-wheel vehicle, so as to improve the carrying capacity and the obstacle crossing ability of the vehicle during moving transversely.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>2</b> and FIG. <b>4</b>, the two transverse driving wheels <b>201</b> are respectively arranged on both ends of a transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b>. The transverse driving wheels <b>201</b> at both ends can be driven to rotate through the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b>, and the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b> is provided with a transverse coupling. In order to improve the moving stability of the vehicle, the two third transverse driven wheels <b>204</b> are connected through a transverse connecting shaft <b>209</b> to ensure that the third transverse driven wheels <b>204</b> on both sides can run synchronously. Additionally, the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b> is connected to the transverse motor <b>208</b> through a first transverse transmission belt <b>207</b>, the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b> is driven to rotate by the transverse motor <b>208</b> through the first transverse transmission belt <b>207</b>, and the transverse motor <b>208</b> is a servo drive motor.

The working principle of the transverse movement drive mechanism <b>200</b> in the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle is as follows: the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b> is driven to rotate by the transverse motor <b>208</b> through the first transverse transmission belt <b>207</b>, the transverse driving wheels <b>201</b> at both ends of the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b> are simultaneously driven to rotate; and, the first transverse driven wheels <b>202</b>, the second transverse driven wheels <b>203</b> and the third transverse driven wheels <b>204</b> on the support frame <b>100</b> are synchronously driven to rotate, so as to realize the transverse moving of the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle.

In some preferred embodiments, referring to FIG. <b>3</b> and FIG. <b>4</b>, the longitudinal movement drive mechanism <b>300</b> acts as an execution unit for a longitudinal movement of the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle. The longitudinal movement drive mechanism <b>300</b> includes first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b>, second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b>, first longitudinal driven wheels <b>303</b> and second longitudinal driven wheels <b>304</b>, in which each set of the wheels is symmetrically arranged on the left and right sides of the support frame <b>100</b>. Each set of the wheels has two wheels symmetrically arranged on both sides of the support frame <b>100</b> to form a structure of a one-way eight-wheel vehicle, so as to improve the carrying capacity and the obstacle crossing ability of the vehicle during moving longitudinally.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>3</b> and FIG. <b>4</b>, the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b> on both sides are used as driving wheels and are respectively connected to a longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b> through first longitudinal transmission belts <b>308</b> on both sides, and the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b> is connected to the longitudinal motor <b>307</b> through a second longitudinal transmission belt <b>306</b>. The longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b> is driven to rotate by the longitudinal motor <b>307</b> through the second longitudinal transmission belt <b>306</b>, and the longitudinal motor <b>307</b> is a servo drive motor.

In one example, in order to match sixteen wheels, the transmission mechanism of the longitudinal wheels is optimized. The position of the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b> is moved up, so that the assembling structure of the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b>, the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b> can form a triangular structure on its side. On the one hand, the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b> are driven to rotate synchronously through the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b>. On the other hand, the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b> arranged in an elevated position can be well staggered from the reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b> arranged below it, which makes the structure of the vehicle more compact, reasonable and space-saving.

In one example, in order to make the vehicle have a better carrying capacity, the diameter of the eight longitudinal wheels used in the longitudinal movement drive mechanism <b>300</b> is larger than the diameter of the eight transverse wheels used in the transverse movement drive mechanism <b>200</b>. Specifically, the diameters of the transverse driving wheels <b>201</b>, the first transverse driven wheels <b>202</b>, the second transverse driven wheels <b>203</b> and the third transverse driven wheels <b>204</b> are all slightly smaller than the diameters of the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b>, the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b>, the first longitudinal driven wheels <b>303</b> and the second longitudinal driven wheels <b>304</b>. The diameter ratio is about 1:1.1-1:1.3; preferably, 1:1.2.

The working principle of the longitudinal movement drive mechanism <b>300</b> in the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle is as follows: the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b> is driven to rotate by the longitudinal motor <b>307</b> through the second longitudinal transmission belt <b>306</b>, the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b> at both ends of the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b> are simultaneously driven to rotate; and, the first longitudinal driven wheels <b>303</b> and the second longitudinal driven wheels <b>304</b> on the support frame <b>100</b> are synchronously driven to rotate, so as to realize the longitudinal moving of the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle. Additionally, a longitudinal coupling is arranged on the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b>. Moreover, the second longitudinal transmission belt <b>306</b> will be re-tensioned after working for a period of time, and the adjustment can be achieved by a transverse movement of the tension adjustment mechanism <b>700</b> according to the tightness of the pulley.

In some preferred embodiments, referring to FIG. <b>4</b>, FIG. <b>7</b>, FIG. <b>8</b>, FIG. <b>9</b>, FIG. <b>10</b> and FIG. <b>11</b>, the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b> acts as an execution unit for a jacking and reversing movement of the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle. The cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b> includes jacking transverse plates <b>401</b> symmetrically arranged on the front and rear sides of the support frame <b>100</b>, and cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b> respectively arranged at the bottoms of both ends of each jacking transverse plate <b>401</b>; each end of the jacking transverse plate <b>401</b> is provided with a first reversing assembling groove <b>414</b> and a second reversing assembling groove <b>415</b>, to assemble the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b> and the transverse connecting shaft <b>209</b>; each cable-stayed jacking block <b>402</b> is provided with a jacking shaft hole <b>410</b> penetrating left and right and a jacking inclined hole <b>411</b> arranging obliquely and penetrating the front and rear; a ball bearing sleeve <b>407</b> is movably arranged in the jacking shaft hole <b>410</b>; a cable-stayed jacking shaft <b>409</b> is arranged between the front and rear sides of the ball bearing sleeve <b>407</b>, and the cable-stayed jacking shaft <b>409</b> is movably embedded in the jacking inclined hole <b>411</b>; and, a ball screw <b>403</b> is movably penetrated in each ball bearing sleeve <b>407</b>, the cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b> at both ends are connected with each other through the ball screws <b>403</b>, and the ball screws <b>403</b> are drivingly connected to the transverse motor <b>208</b> through a chain <b>406</b>.

The working principle of the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b> in the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle is as follows: the ball screws <b>403</b> on the front and rear sides are driven to rotate synchronously by the transverse motor <b>208</b> through the chain <b>406</b>. During the rotation of the ball screws <b>403</b>, the ball bearing sleeve <b>407</b> and the two cable-stayed jacking shafts <b>409</b> are driven to slide left and right along the jacking shaft hole <b>410</b> and the jacking inclined hole <b>411</b> respectively, and the cable-stayed jacking block <b>402</b> is driven to move up and down. Then the jacking transverse plates <b>401</b> on the top of the cable-stayed jacking shaft <b>409</b> and the jacking platform provided on the jacking transverse plates <b>401</b> are driven to lift up and down, thus realizing the functions of jacking and reversing.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>4</b> and FIG. <b>7</b>, two ends of the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b> and two ends of the transverse connection shaft <b>209</b> are respectively arranged passing through the first reversing assembling grooves <b>414</b> and the second reversing assembling grooves <b>415</b> at both ends of the corresponding jacking transverse plate <b>401</b>, so that the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b> and the transverse connection shaft <b>209</b> can move up and down synchronously along with the jacking transverse plate <b>401</b>. Then, the eight transverse wheels of the transverse drive mechanism <b>200</b>, consisting of the transverse driving wheels <b>201</b>, the first transverse driven wheels <b>202</b>, the second transverse driven wheels <b>203</b> and the third transverse driven wheels <b>204</b>, are driven to move up and down synchronously relative to the eight longitudinal wheels, so as to leave the ground or contact the ground, thereby realizing the reversing function between the transverse movement drive mechanism <b>200</b> and the longitudinal movement drive mechanism <b>300</b>.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>7</b> and FIG. <b>9</b>, the cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b> on the same side are linked by the ball screws <b>403</b>, and the two cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b> are respectively arranged on both ends of the ball screws <b>403</b>. The middle position of each ball screw <b>403</b> is sleeved with a gear bearing sleeve <b>405</b>, and the gear bearing sleeve <b>405</b> is connected to the transverse motor <b>208</b> through the chain <b>406</b> and the cross reducer <b>210</b>. After the transverse motor <b>208</b> is decelerated and reversed by the cross reducer <b>210</b>, the torque of the ball screw <b>403</b> is controlled after being driven by the chain <b>406</b>. Then the cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b> at both ends are driven to lift up and down by the ball screw <b>403</b>, so that the jacking transverse plates <b>401</b> and the jacking platform provided on the jacking transverse plates <b>401</b> are driven to lift up and down, thus realizing the functions of jacking and reversing.

In some preferred embodiments, referring to FIG. <b>5</b>, the supporting frame <b>100</b> is an integral frame formed by the connecting between a bending sheet metal and local reinforcing ribs. The front and rear side walls of the support frame <b>100</b> are respectively provided with four square mounting holes <b>101</b> to assemble the transverse wheels, and correspondingly, the left and right side walls of the support frame <b>100</b> are respectively provided with four circular mounting holes <b>102</b> to assemble the longitudinal wheels.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>5</b> and FIG. <b>7</b>, the transverse driving wheels <b>201</b> on the front and rear sides are connected with the transverse transmission shaft <b>206</b> which passes through the second reversing assembling groove <b>415</b> to assemble into the corresponding square assembling holes <b>101</b>; the axles of the first transverse driven wheels <b>202</b> and the second transverse driven wheels <b>203</b> respectively pass through the corresponding square mounting holes <b>101</b> to connect with the corresponding jacking transverse plates <b>401</b>; and, the third transverse driven wheels <b>204</b> are connected with the transverse connecting shaft <b>209</b> which passes through the first reversing assembling groove <b>414</b> to assemble into the corresponding square assembling holes <b>101</b>. Moreover, in order to cooperate with the jacking and reversing functions of the reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b>, the transverse driving wheels <b>201</b>, the first transverse driven wheels <b>202</b>, the second transverse driven wheels <b>203</b> and the third transverse driven wheels <b>204</b> along with the jacking transverse plates <b>401</b> can be lifted between the upper and lower limit positions in the corresponding square assembling holes <b>101</b>.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>4</b>, FIG. <b>5</b> and FIG. <b>6</b>, the axles of the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b>, which respectively pass through the corresponding circular mounting holes <b>102</b>, are connected to the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b>. The first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b> are driven to rotate synchronously by the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b> through the first longitudinal belts <b>308</b> to improve the carrying capacity of the vehicle. In addition, the axles of the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b> are respectively arranged passing through the corresponding circular assembling holes <b>102</b> and are fixedly connected to the bottom of the support frame <b>100</b> through first bearing seats <b>105</b>, so as to ensure the stability of the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b> during rotating.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>3</b>, FIG. <b>4</b>, FIG. <b>5</b> and FIG. <b>6</b>, the first longitudinal driven wheels <b>303</b> and the second longitudinal driven wheels <b>304</b> are used as driven wheels, and are driven passively by the support frame <b>100</b> to mainly have the function of supporting and guiding. The first longitudinal driven wheels <b>303</b> and the second longitudinal driven wheels <b>304</b> are fixed in the corresponding circular mounting holes <b>102</b>.

In some preferred embodiments, referring to FIG. <b>2</b>, FIG. <b>4</b> and FIG. <b>7</b>, the wheel installation structure of the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle is optimized. The first transverse driven wheels <b>202</b> are used as the auxiliary driving wheels of the vehicle, which further improves the transverse gripping ability and obstacle crossing ability of the vehicle. Specifically, the transverse driving wheel <b>201</b> and the first transverse driven wheel <b>202</b> on the same side are connected with each other through the second transverse transmission belt <b>205</b>. The power of the transverse driving wheels <b>201</b> is transmitted to the first transverse driven wheels <b>202</b> through the second transverse transmission belt <b>205</b>, and the first transverse driven wheels <b>202</b> can be driven to rotate synchronously.

In addition, according to the actual operation requirements of the vehicle, the transverse driving wheel <b>201</b> and the third transverse driven wheel <b>204</b> on the same side are connected with each other through the second transverse transmission belt <b>205</b>. The third transverse driven wheels <b>204</b> are used as a linkage mechanism of the transverse driving wheels <b>201</b>, thereby further improving the stability, the ground gripping ability and obstacle crossing ability during the transverse moving of the vehicle.

In some preferred embodiments, referring to FIG. <b>1</b> and FIG. <b>5</b>, in order to ensure the stability of the power transmission of the longitudinal movement drive mechanism <b>300</b>, the sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle further comprises a tension adjusting mechanism <b>700</b> positioned outside each of the first longitudinal transmission belt <b>308</b>. The connection between the first longitudinal transmission belts <b>308</b>, the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the second longitudinal driving wheels <b>302</b> is optimized through the tension adjustment mechanism <b>700</b>, so that the installation of the first longitudinal transmission belts <b>308</b> can be more convenient.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>6</b>, each tension adjusting mechanism <b>700</b> includes a first tensioning pulley <b>701</b> and a second tensioning pulley <b>703</b> which are both attached to the outside of the corresponding first longitudinal transmission belt <b>308</b>. The first tensioning pulley <b>701</b> is fixedly arranged on the inner side wall of the support frame <b>100</b> through a first fixing plate <b>702</b>. The first tensioning pulley <b>701</b> is fixed to be always located between the first longitudinal driving wheels <b>301</b> and the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b>, and to be always in contact with the first longitudinal transmission belt <b>308</b>. The second tensioning pulley <b>703</b> is movably arranged on the inner side wall of the support frame <b>100</b> through an adjustment plate <b>704</b>. With the adjusting function of the adjusting plate <b>704</b> to the second tensioning pulley <b>703</b>, the second tensioning pulley <b>703</b> can have a relative displacement to the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b>, so as to press tightly to fit against the other side end surface of the first longitudinal transmission belt <b>308</b> to form a tensioned state for the first longitudinal transmission belt <b>308</b>. Alternatively, the second tensioning pulley <b>703</b> moves away from the longitudinal transmission shaft <b>305</b> to separate it from the first longitudinal transmission belt <b>308</b>, thus the tensioned state of the first longitudinal transmission belt <b>308</b> can be eliminated.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>6</b>, the adjusting plate <b>704</b> is provided with an adjusting square hole <b>705</b> in horizontal. The adjusting plate <b>704</b>, passing through the adjusting square hole <b>705</b>, is movably fixed to the inner side wall of the supporting frame <b>100</b> by bolts. The adjusting square hole <b>705</b> has a certain limiting function under the action of the bolts. The left and right adjustable spacing of the second tensioning pulley <b>703</b> is determined by the length of the adjusting square hole <b>705</b>, to meet the tensioning requirement of the first longitudinal transmission belt <b>308</b>.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>6</b>, a distal end of the adjusting plate <b>704</b> is adjustably connected to a second fixing plate <b>706</b> by adjustable nuts and bolts. In the presence of adjusting nuts and bolts, the adjusting plate <b>704</b> and the second tensioning pulley <b>703</b> arranged on the adjusting plate <b>704</b> can be controlled to move left and right along the length direction of the adjusting square hole <b>705</b>, thereby adjusting the tension of the first longitudinal transmission belt <b>308</b>. The second fixing plate <b>706</b> is fixed on the inner side wall of the supporting frame <b>100</b>. The adjusting plate <b>704</b> and the second fixing plate <b>706</b> are both L-shaped plates, and their side end surfaces are arranged oppositely. The adjusting nuts and bolts are located on the oppositely arranged L-shaped plates. The adjustment of the adjusting nuts and bolts can be achieved by the existing conventional structure, which will not be detailed described here.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>4</b>, FIG. <b>7</b>, FIG. <b>8</b> and FIG. <b>9</b>, the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b> further comprises second bearing seats <b>404</b>, each of which is arranged on the support frame <b>100</b> with a bearing arranged at its top to support the corresponding ball screw <b>403</b>. There are at least two second bearing seats <b>404</b> on each side of the support frame <b>100</b>, which are respectively disposed between and/or on both sides of the cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b>. Each ball screw <b>403</b> is installed in a mounting hole on the upper end of the corresponding second bearing seat <b>404</b> through a bearing. The two ends of the ball screw <b>403</b> both protrude out of the cable-stayed jacking block <b>402</b> and pass through the bearing installed in the mounting hole. The second bearing seat <b>404</b> has the functions of stabilizing and supporting the ball screw <b>403</b>, thus improving the stability of the operation of the cable-stayed reversing jacking assembly.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>4</b>, FIG. <b>7</b>, FIG. <b>9</b> and FIG. <b>11</b>, the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b> further comprises jacking nuts <b>408</b>, each of which is fixedly sleeved on the ball bearing sleeve <b>407</b> with its front and rear side walls respectively welded and connected to the cable-stayed jacking shaft <b>409</b>. The jacking nut <b>408</b> is a copper nut, and each jacking nut <b>408</b> is fixedly sleeved on the corresponding ball bearing sleeve <b>407</b> with a 50%-70% transmission efficiency, which reduces the kinetic energy loss.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>8</b> and FIG. <b>9</b>, the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b> further comprises limiting sliding grooves <b>412</b> and limiting guide posts <b>413</b>, in which the limiting sliding grooves <b>412</b> are respectively vertically arranged on the front and rear side walls of the cable-stayed jacking block <b>402</b>, and the limiting guide posts <b>413</b> are vertically arranged on the support frame <b>100</b> and slidably connected with the limiting sliding grooves <b>412</b>. The limiting guide posts <b>413</b> are used to limit the horizontal movement of the cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b> and play a role of vertical guidance simultaneously. During the movement of the ball bearing sleeve <b>307</b>, in order to prevent the cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b> from moving due to the horizontal pulling force, the horizontal pulling force received by the cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b> is converted to the vertical pulling force through the jacking inclined holes <b>411</b>, so that the cable-stayed jacking blocks <b>402</b> can move up and down along the limiting guide posts <b>413</b>.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>4</b> and FIG. <b>7</b>, in order to ensure the stability of the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism <b>400</b> during the jacking and reversing process, each jacking transverse plate <b>401</b> is connected to corresponding jacking guide posts <b>103</b> by sliding up and down through guide sliding grooves <b>416</b>, the guide sliding grooves <b>416</b> are opened at both ends of the jacking transverse plate <b>401</b>, and the jacking guide posts <b>103</b> are located at the four corners of the support frame <b>100</b>. Both ends of the jacking transverse plate <b>401</b> can slide along the jacking guide posts <b>103</b> during lifting, to avoid the defect that the jacking transverse plate <b>401</b> sways from side to side during jacking. The upper and lower ends of the jacking guide post <b>103</b> are fixedly connected to the support frame <b>100</b> through guide post fixing plates <b>104</b> respectively. The guide post fixing plates <b>104</b> provided at the upper and lower ends simultaneously play a limiting role in the lifting and lowering of the jacking transverse plate <b>401</b>.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>11</b>, according to the implementation requirements, different inclination angles of the jacking inclined hole <b>411</b> can be designed for different working conditions. The inclination angle of the jacking inclined hole <b>411</b> is 5-85°; preferably, 15-75°; more preferably, 25-65°; further preferably, 30-65°; furthermore preferably, 35-65°; most preferably, 40-45°.

In one example, referring to FIG. <b>9</b> and FIG. <b>11</b>, the jacking shaft hole <b>410</b> is communicated with the jacking inclined hole <b>411</b>. The shape of the jacking shaft hole <b>410</b> and the jacking inclined hole <b>411</b> is designed in the structure of an arc slot hole. The vertical height of the jacking shaft hole <b>410</b> is equal to the vertical height of the jacking inclined hole <b>411</b>, so as to synchronously satisfy the requirement of the up and down movement of the ball screw <b>403</b> in the jacking shaft hole <b>410</b> and the up and down movement of the cable-stayed jacking shaft <b>409</b> in the jacking inclined hole <b>411</b>, during the up and down moving of the jacking blocks.

The cable-stayed jacking reversing type sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle in the present disclosure uses eight wheels on each side to form a structure of a sixteen-wheel two-way moving vehicle, which greatly improves the carrying capacity and obstacle crossing ability of the vehicle. Using the cable-stayed reversing jacking mechanism, the cable-stayed jacking blocks contact the rolling line of the cable-stayed jacking shaft, which makes the transmission efficiency high with an un-changed pressure angle. The jacking inclined holes on both sides of the jacking block are symmetrically arranged with a uniform force. The structure of the vehicle is novel and compact with stable operation, small size and high carrying capacity, which reduces the maintenance and maintenance times of the mechanical mechanism so as to have a high working efficiency and a long service life. The cable-stayed jacking reversing type sixteen-wheel four-way shuttle has a compact structure, which not only reduces costs and saves space, but also reduces the overall weight and volume of the vehicle and improves the running capacity and cargo storage efficiency.
